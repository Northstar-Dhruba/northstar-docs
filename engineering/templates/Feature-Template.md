# Feature Template

## Problem Statement

Describe the problem or business need that motivates the feature.

## Business Requirements

List the required business outcomes.

## Architecture Impact

Describe any impact on architecture, boundaries, or dependencies.

## Implementation Plan

Summarize the implementation approach.

## Testing Strategy

Describe the test approach for the feature.

## Acceptance Criteria

List the conditions that must be satisfied for completion.

## Risks

Identify significant risks or constraints.

## Related ADRs

List any related ADRs or design decisions.

## Review Checklist

- [ ] Architecture impact reviewed
- [ ] Domain impact reviewed
- [ ] Tests defined
- [ ] Documentation updated
- [ ] Acceptance criteria clear
