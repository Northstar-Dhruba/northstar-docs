# Domain Modeling Principles

## Purpose

This document summarizes architectural principles established during Foundation development.

## Principles

- Reference-First Engineering
- Business Families
- Engineering References
- Reference Implementation Registry
- ADR-based architectural evolution
- Separation of business semantics from implementation

## Interpretation

- Business semantics define what a model means.
- Engineering references define how approved patterns are inherited.
- The registry is the authoritative source of implementation references.
- ADRs govern architectural evolution.

## Foundation Context

Foundation is complete and stable.

Core Domain development should build on Foundation rather than reworking Foundation semantics.
